                        Backup

                        Jobs, candidates and notes are saved only on this computer. Download a backup to keep a copy or move to another computer. The file contains candidate data: keep it somewhere safe. Settings and your app token aren’t in it.

                        How to use Debrief
                        1. Jobs: add a job, paste its description and make the skills checklist. Add each candidate with their profile link, interview date and resume.
                        2. Interview (first tab): opens on the interview that’s running, or your next candidate. Upcoming lists everyone due; tap someone to see their page, then “Interview now” to make them current.
                        3. Plan: make an interview plan: what their resume shows for each skill and what to ask.
                        4. Interview: open the call in a Chrome tab, click the Debrief icon on that tab, tell the candidate the call is transcribed, then press Start interview: listening starts with it, and you can stop and restart it at any time. Tick skills as you cover them and write notes.
                        5. Notes: after you end the interview, your notes are kept there to read or copy. Debrief never scores candidates.
